## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an intentional, editorial creative workspace engineered for high-output content creators, solo entrepreneurs, and brand marketing teams. The aesthetic rejects decorative AI gimmicks—deliberately avoiding saturated purple-to-cyan gradients, neon glow artifacts, and frosted glassy layers—in favor of a disciplined, tactile studio environment. 

The visual style blends **Modern Minimalist Editorial** with **High-Utility Studio Architecture**:
- **Clarity over novelty:** Every border, container, and visual anchor serves to frame the creator's assets, not compete with them.
- **Quiet intelligence:** AI affordances are treated as precise precision instruments—crisp glyphs, micro-badges, and understated status dots—rather than magical sparkles.
- **Tactile confidence:** Surfaces rely on calibrated off-white layers, hairline architectural strokes (1px), and deep typographic contrast to instill trust during high-speed content batching.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authoritative, calm backdrop that emphasizes uploaded photo assets and editorial copy.

- **Primary Brand Accent (`#1E293B`):** Deep Navy/Slate anchor used for primary calls-to-action, active sidebar states, and definitive chrome framing.
- **Interactive Action Blue (`#2563EB`):** A measured cobalt used exclusively for active text highlights, processing indicators, selection rings, and generation trigger states.
- **Semantic Support:**
  - **Success / Generation Stable (`#10B981`):** Subdued emerald with light tint backing (`#ECFDF5`) for verified status flags and publish confirmations.
  - **Attention / Verification Alert (`#F59E0B`):** Warm amber with subtle background (`#FEF3C7`) signaling aspect-ratio clipping, token limits, or human review warnings.
  - **Platform Sync Dot (`#3B82F6`):** Pure electric slate for live-synced canvas nodes and draft version tags.
- **Canvas & Surface Layers:**
  - **Base Background:** `#F8FAFC` (Slate-50) for foundational desktop chrome.
  - **Card / Canvas Surface:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) providing absolute contrast against photos.
  - **Subtle Partition Borders:** `#E2E8F0` (Hairline structural boundary) and `#F1F5F9` (Nested item divider).
- **Typographic Neutral Spectrum:**
  - **Primary Ink:** `#0F172A` (Rich Slate-900) for uncompromised scannability.
  - **Secondary Supporting:** `#475569` (Slate-600) for meta specs, input labels, and inactive tab handles.
  - **Muted Placeholder:** `#94A3B8` (Slate-400) for structural guides, empty states, and inactive counters.

## Typography

Typography relies on **Inter** configured for strict metric legibility and neutral editorial rhythm. It ensures that user-generated copy across Instagram captions, long-form blog drafts, and carousel panels maintains typographic clarity.

- **Display & Section Titles:** Tight negative letter-spacing (`-0.025em` to `-0.015em`) removes excess tracking from digital screens, lending headings a carved, print-grade composure.
- **Platform Previews:** The `body-lg` and `body-md` configurations maintain a generous line height (`1.5` to `1.625`) to accurately reflect real-world social feed viewing conditions without visual crowding.
- **Micro-Chrome & Badges:** `label-sm` and `caption-code` are tuned for asset counts, slide IDs (`01/08`), and aspect-ratio indicators (`4:5`, `16:9`), utilizing slightly elevated letter tracking (`0.02em` - `0.04em`) to ensure legibility at scale.

## Layout & Spacing

The workspace implements a **modular docked workbench architecture** optimized for desktop creation while collapsing gracefully onto touch viewports.

### Breakpoints & Fluidity
- **Desktop Workbench (`>= 1280px`):** Fixed-width persistent navigation sidebar (260px), central flexible asset stage or editor canvas (fluid flex-1), and collapsible contextual action sidebar (340px). Gutters set strictly to `1.5rem`.
- **Tablet Workspace (`768px - 1279px`):** Collapsible icon rail navigation (64px), single-column platform workspace with horizontal bottom sheet drawer for thumbnail staging. Gutters adapt to `1rem`.
- **Mobile Handheld (`< 768px`):** Single full-width canvas viewport, sticky top contextual switcher tabs, and bottom sheet action drawers. Horizontal margins compress to `1rem`.

### Rhythmic Scale
Layout compositions adhere to an exact **4px base grid** where internal card padding relies on `space-md` (16px) or `space-lg` (24px) to retain an uncluttered, breathable gallery feel. Dense inspector sidebars compress element stacking to `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy operates through **hairline outlines and disciplined micro-shadows**, strictly avoiding diffused blurry elevations or glassmorphism.

- **Level 0 (Base Bed):** Flat `#F8FAFC` foundation with zero shadow. All secondary workspace regions rest here.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** White surface backed by a 1px solid border in `#E2E8F0` and an ultra-subtle grounding shadow: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Canvases & Floating Overlays):** Interactive drag cards, floating format bars, and platform tab bars use: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` enclosed within a `#E2E8F0` perimeter border.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Verification Popovers):** High-precision popovers sit above active workspaces using: `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)` with a neutral crisp boundary (`#CBD5E1`).

## Shapes

The design system enforces a **calibrated 10px to 14px radius range** (`roundedness: 2`) that softens administrative density while retaining structural editorial authority.

- **Standard Cards & Canvases (`rounded-lg` / `12px`):** Used for platform canvas panels, image cards, and sidebar containers.
- **Inputs & Action Pills (`rounded` / `8px`):** Form controls, contextual AI triggers, search fields, and buttons feature tight, controlled corners.
- **Badge Indicators & Counters (`rounded-full` / `9999px`):** Image index pills, status dots, and aspect-ratio indicators retain pure circular geometry to contrast immediately against rectangular photographic assets.

## Components

### Buttons & Contextual Action Pills
- **Primary Studio Action:** `#1E293B` background, pure white text, 8px radius, height of 40px. Subtle hover darkening to `#0F172A`. Never uses gradients.
- **AI Transform Pills:** Surface `#F8FAFC`, hairline stroke `#E2E8F0`, typography `label-sm` in `#1E293B`. Features a quiet cobalt leading icon (`#2563EB`). Hover state shifts stroke to `#CBD5E1` and adds subtle shadow Level 1.
- **Ghost Utility Action:** Transparent background, slate text (`#475569`), hover background `#F1F5F9`.

### Multi-Image Asset Gallery Grid
- **Photo Cards:** 12px radius, `#FFFFFF` interior border, containing image thumbnail set to `object-fit: cover`.
- **Drag Reorder Handle:** Top-left 6-dot matrix icon in muted `#94A3B8`, visible on hover or during drag operations.
- **Index Tag:** Top-right circular pill (`caption-code`), slate translucent backing (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.75)`), white text (`01`, `02`).
- **Aspect Ratio Guide:** Bottom edge indicator depicting crop bounds per target platform.

### Platform Workspace Tab Bar
- Pill-segmented design inside a `#F1F5F9` tray.
- Tabs for **Blog**, **Instagram**, **Facebook**, and **Carousel**.
- Selected state adopts `#FFFFFF` elevation, Level 1 micro-shadow, `#0F172A` text weight 600, with no heavy underlines.

### Verification Badges & Contextual Popovers
- **Amber Warning Badge:** `#FEF3C7` background, `#B45309` text, `#F59E0B` 1.5px icon outline.
- **Interaction Popover:** Click-activated container with Level 3 elevation, detailing the specific remediation needed (e.g., *"Instagram Carousel requires 4:5 crop to prevent auto-truncation"*), accompanied by a single-click "Apply Auto-Crop" pill.

### Slide Thumbnail Track
- Horizontal strip positioned beneath or beside the active preview canvas.
- 68x68px aspect-ratio cards with active item indicated by an unbroken 2px `#2563EB` border offset by 2px white space.

### Form Inputs & Caption Editors
- Border 1px `#E2E8F0`, interior `#FFFFFF`, 8px radius, text `body-md` in `#0F172A`.
- Active focus state: zero neon glow; sharp 1px ring in `#2563EB` with matching border color.
- Character count tracker docked to bottom right in `caption-code` slate (`#94A3B8`).
